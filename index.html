Decisions, not data

Why I changed what a Ziki issue page shows a person, and when.

Intent brief

WhoThe person assigned to a Ziki issue, or who owns the agents working on it. Logs in once or twice a day, between other work.
Intent"Tell me if my issue is moving, and if anything needs me."
EmotionUnsure and a bit anxious. I found out a day late that an agent had rejected my issue, and I couldn't tell if my fix was stuck or fine.
DecisionDo I need to act on this issue right now, and what exactly do I do?
Old UIShows "Open · Ready" and a raw thread. The answer is spread across comments 3, 4 and 6, so the person reads everything to learn three facts.
ChangeA situation summary at the top of the issue: where it is on Ziki's path, what the agents concluded (each linked to its comment), and only the items that need a person, with one-click actions.

Who does what: agent alone vs person decides

Agents do aloneConfirm the issue is real, set severity, review the fix, check production, link related issues, write the summary itself. These show under "Handled without you".
Person must decideMoving code between organisations (sending a team PR to central), settling conflicting agent verdicts, accepting or refusing scope changes an agent suggests, and closing the issue. These show under "Needs you".
Why the line sits hereZiki's own promise is "Agents ask before they act. People make the call." Anything that changes what ships, or overrules an agent, is a call for a person. Anything that only gathers or checks facts is agent work.

Where the change lives: the issue page

Every Ziki issue collects verdicts and comments from many agents. Today the page shows a status (Open, Ready) and the raw thread. To know what is going on, the person reads every comment. On my own #46 that meant 6 comments and 11 decisions to learn three facts: my fix was approved, it was not live, and one agent had rejected it.

The situation summary sits at the top of the issue and answers: where is this on Ziki's path, what did the agents conclude, and what needs me. Each fact links to the comment it came from, so nothing is hidden and the thread stays one click away. When I act, the summary updates.

Rules for the summary: show the furthest step reached and mark the step that is blocked; raise "Needs you" only when the next step needs a person, when verdicts conflict, or when an agent suggests a change; fold everything else into "Handled without you".

The Desk tab shows the same idea across all issues, as a bonus.

The promise Ziki already makes

Ziki's homepage says: "Agents ask before they act. People make the call." and "From report to merge, nothing happens in the dark." Inside the app, the person still works like a ticket clerk: totals, lists, a points chart and four separate places that might hold something for them.

What I saw using Ziki as a manager this week

Whenever I log into Zedu, it takes me straight to my messages. I rarely chat there, so that isn't what matters to me. I want to land on the few things I actually get information from, like the announcement and project channels. Ziki has the same habit: it opens on totals and lists, not on what needs me.

When I saw my 5 agents marked "Idle, Standing by" and the Inbox saying "Nothing here right now", I felt bad. I couldn't tell if they were broken, blocked or simply finished, and nothing told me what to do next.

I only found out today (26 Oct) that Bolu Triager had rejected my issue #46. The last time I opened that thread was Thursday or Friday last week. In between, one agent approved my fix, another confirmed it still isn't live, and another rejected it with a bug template. Nothing brought any of that to me. That is the problem Ziki Desk solves.

The perspective I chose

The person is a manager of agents. A manager doesn't read every log. They want three answers: what needs my decision, is the work moving, and which agent needs me and why. Everything else can wait in a digest.

What changed in the interaction

  • One queue instead of four places. Escalations, inbox items, stale assigned issues and agent conflicts merge into one list, ranked by what it costs to wait.
  • A situation, not a thread. Each item shows where the work sits on Ziki's own path (Report, Confirm, Authorize, Deliver, Prove, Merge), the facts that matter with who said them, and the agent's suggested next step. The raw thread stays one click away.
  • Conflicts surface. When agents disagree (10 confirm, 1 rejects), that becomes a decision for the person instead of a comment buried at the bottom.
  • Silence is explained. "Idle, Standing by" becomes "idle because their work ran out", with an optional action. Quiet now means fine, not unknown.
  • Nothing disappears silently. Every decision lands in "Done today", and routine agent work folds into "Handled without you".

What I deliberately kept

The visual style is the same as today's Ziki. The redesign is about which information reaches the person and the actions they take, not colours or layout.

What it needs from Ziki's API

Everything shown already exists in Ziki: escalations, inbox items, issue validations and verdicts, assignee, PR link, comments with agent attribution, and the points ledger. The Desk ranks and summarises them. Rules used here: raise an item when the next step needs a person, when agent verdicts conflict, or when an issue assigned to you hasn't moved in 5 days.
