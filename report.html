1. The marketing fix: pricing plan names

Problem on zedu.chat/pricing (checked 26 Oct): the plans are called Starter, Pro and Enterprise, but the Pro card says "Everything in Zedu Free", Enterprise says "Everything in Zedu Educator", and the comparison table calls Pro "Growth". Six names for three plans confuses people at the moment they decide to pay.

Tickets: Ziki #554 (open, confirmed) and #413 (marked resolved, but its linked fix zedu-hng/zedu-fe #785 was never merged: it comes from a fork not registered in team routing, open since 9 Oct).

Finding: the corrected text ("Everything in Starter / Pro") is already in central dev and central-staging. Nobody needs to write it again. It only needs to be released.
